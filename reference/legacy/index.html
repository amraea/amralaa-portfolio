Professional Profile

I am a Financial and Data Analyst with over 14 years of experience bridging the gap between finance and technology. My career spans comprehensive financial analysis, budgeting, enterprise reporting, and advanced data analytics, supported by deep expertise in SAP S/4HANA FI and business intelligence tools.

Starting my career at Cairo Oil Refining Company, I built a strong foundation in financial operations, budgeting, and strategic planning.
